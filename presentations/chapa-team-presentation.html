Affordable Housing Applications

Geographic, demographic, and pricing patterns in affordable-homeownership applications

Overview

What the team studied

The project explored how applications varied across properties, towns, age groups, bedroom types, and applicant demographics in the Massachusetts affordable housing market.

My role

My contribution

I focused on data cleaning and exploratory data analysis, including combining the application datasets and examining application patterns across age, household size, bedroom count, and location.

Questions

Client questions addressed

  • Did application patterns differ by age group?
  • Did applicants request different bedroom types by age or household size?
  • How did demand vary across towns and properties?
  • How did applicant composition vary by property and location?
Findings

What the EDA showed

Application patterns differed meaningfully by age and housing characteristics. The distributions suggested that age-restricted and non-age-restricted properties were attracting different applicant profiles, with visible variation in town-level demand and bedroom type demand.

My work focused on data cleaning and EDA; other team members developed the Bayesian model and interactive map for the project deliverable.
